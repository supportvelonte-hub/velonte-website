Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Shipping Policy explains how VELONTE processes and delivers orders placed through our website.

1. Order Processing

Orders are normally processed after successful payment confirmation. Processing time can vary depending on product availability, order volume and verification requirements.

2. Delivery

We aim to deliver orders within the delivery estimate shown or communicated for the destination. Actual delivery time may vary due to courier operations, weather, holidays, remote locations or other circumstances outside our control.

3. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, will be displayed before you complete payment. Any promotional free-shipping offer will be subject to the conditions stated with that offer.

4. Address Accuracy

Please provide a complete address, PIN code and working mobile number. If an order cannot be delivered because the address is incorrect or the recipient is unavailable, additional delivery attempts or charges may apply where applicable.

5. Damaged Packages

If the package appears seriously damaged at delivery, document the package and product condition with photographs/video where possible and contact VELONTE promptly. Return eligibility is subject to our Return & Refund Policy.

6. Delivery Delays

Courier delays do not automatically qualify an order for a refund if the order is ultimately delivered. If an order is significantly delayed or appears lost, contact us so we can investigate with the courier.

7. Contact

Shipping questions can be sent to contact@velonte.in.
