Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VELONTE collects, uses, stores and protects information when you visit our website or place an order.

1. Information We Collect

  • Name, mobile number, email address and delivery address when you place an order or contact us.
  • Order details such as products, sizes, quantities, prices and order status.
  • Payment information required to process your transaction. VELONTE does not need to store your complete card, UPI or banking credentials; payment processing is handled by our payment service provider.
  • Basic technical information such as browser/device information, pages visited and approximate usage information where required for website operation and security.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, confirm and deliver orders.
  • To communicate with you about your order, delivery, payment or support request.
  • To provide customer support and handle returns, refunds or complaints.
  • To prevent fraud, misuse and security incidents.
  • To improve our website, products and customer experience.

3. Payments

Online payments may be processed through Razorpay or another payment provider selected by VELONTE. Payment providers may collect and process payment information under their own privacy policies and security practices. VELONTE does not ask customers to share card PINs, passwords, OTPs or UPI PINs with us.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share only the information reasonably necessary to operate the service, such as with delivery/courier partners, payment processors, website/hosting providers and service providers assisting with customer support or security. We do not intend to sell your personal information.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect customer information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to complete orders, provide support, maintain business and transaction records, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes.

7. Cookies

VELONTE may use cookies or similar technologies for essential website functions, preferences, analytics and security. You can control cookies through your browser settings, although disabling some cookies may affect website functionality.

8. Your Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or information about personal data associated with your orders, subject to applicable law and reasonable verification requirements.

9. Children

Our website is intended for general consumers and is not knowingly directed at children. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, practices or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with the updated date.

11. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to contact@velonte.in.
