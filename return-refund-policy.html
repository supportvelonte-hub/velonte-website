Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Return & Refund Policy explains when a VELONTE order may be eligible for return, replacement or refund.

1. Damaged or Incorrect Product

If you receive a damaged, broken, defective or materially incorrect product, contact VELONTE as soon as possible after delivery. For glass products, clear photographs/video of the outer packaging, shipping label and product condition are especially helpful for investigation.

2. Return Request

Return or replacement requests should be sent to contact@velonte.in with the order details, reason for the request and supporting photographs/video where relevant. We may request additional information before approving a return.

3. Eligibility

  • The product should generally be unused and in its original condition and packaging unless the issue itself prevents this.
  • Products damaged after delivery through misuse, improper handling or normal wear may not qualify.
  • Personalized or specially made products may be non-returnable unless they arrive damaged, defective or incorrect.
  • Eligibility is determined based on the circumstances and applicable law.

4. Replacement

Where an eligible issue is confirmed, VELONTE may offer a replacement where stock is available. If replacement is unavailable or otherwise appropriate, a refund may be offered.

5. Refunds

Approved refunds are generally issued to the original payment method or through the applicable payment provider. Processing time may vary depending on the bank or payment provider.

6. Shipping for Returns

If a return is approved because VELONTE sent an incorrect, defective or damaged product, we will communicate the applicable return-shipping process. Do not ship a product back without receiving return instructions from us.

7. Order Cancellation

Cancellation requests should be made before the order is processed or shipped. Once shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible and the applicable return process may apply.

8. Contact

For a return, replacement or refund request, contact contact@velonte.in with your order information.
